# Product — Focus Ghost

## What it is

Focus Ghost is a focus & productivity web app with a haunted twist. A virtual ghost
lives in the user's browser tab. Focus sessions feed it; distractions weaken it.
Streaks and levels evolve it. A "graveyard" heatmap records every focused day.

## Who it is for

- People who procrastinate and want focus to feel rewarding instead of punishing.
- Fans of virtual pets, streaks, and playful productivity (Tamagotchi, GitHub
  contribution graphs, Duolingo streaks).
- Anyone who likes a spooky aesthetic — the app leans into Halloween year-round.

## Core promise

**Your focus has a soul. Feed it, or it fades.** Every minute of real focus visibly
strengthens a creature you care about. Every distraction visibly weakens it.

## Goals

1. A complete Pomodoro timer that is genuinely useful on its own.
2. A ghost whose state is always an honest reflection of the user's recent focus
   behavior — never random, never pay-to-win, never fake.
3. A streak/graveyard system that rewards showing up every day.
4. A polished, coherent, spooky product experience — not a tech demo.
5. Fully client-side: no account, no backend, no tracking, works offline after load.

## Non-goals

- No social features, no leaderboards against other people (local streaks only).
- No monetization, no ads, no accounts.
- No AI features inside the app itself (the AI is in how the app was built, not in
  what it does).
- No mobile app; the web app must simply work well on a phone browser.

## Tone & design language

Dark, foggy, glowing. Deep purples and greens, candle-light amber accents. The ghost
is cute, not scary. Copy is playful and a little macabre ("Your ghost is starving.",
"Another soul saved."). Never gory, never genuinely frightening.
